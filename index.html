How to Read Launch Lab

Launch Lab ratings use a 0–100 scale. Higher scores generally indicate a more favorable fantasy environment. These ratings are not projected fantasy points, projected yards, or guaranteed betting outcomes.

Player Ratings

Outlook V2

Launch Lab’s overall weekly player rating. It combines the model’s major matchup, opportunity, quality, and game-context signals into one 0–100 score. Higher means a stronger overall weekly outlook.

Matchup

How favorable the opposing defense is for the player’s position and expected usage. Higher scores indicate a more favorable defensive matchup.

Opportunity

How strong the player’s expected role and involvement are for the week. This measures how much usable opportunity the player is expected to receive.

Quality

The strength of the player’s underlying performance profile. It helps separate players who simply have volume from players whose performance and efficiency also support the outlook.

Player Game Environment

How favorable the overall game conditions are for this specific player’s fantasy production.

Game and Position Ratings

Overall Game Environment

Launch Lab’s game-level view of how favorable the matchup is for fantasy scoring overall. Higher scores indicate stronger overall fantasy conditions.

How Favorable Is This Matchup?

The QB, RB, WR, and TE boxes show how favorable the matchup is for the offense at each position. Higher scores mean a more favorable matchup for that position. For example, a high RB rating indicates a favorable environment for running backs. These numbers are ratings — not projected fantasy points or yardage.

Usage Lab

Target Share

The percentage of a team's receiving targets earned by the player during the selected sample.

Opportunity Share

The percentage of the team's combined rushing attempts and receiving targets accounted for by the player.

Red Zone Opportunity Share

The percentage of the team's combined rushing attempts and receiving targets inside the opponent's 20-yard line accounted for by the player.

Red Zone Target Share

The percentage of the team's targets inside the opponent's 20-yard line earned by the player.

10-Zone / 5-Zone

Opportunities occurring inside the opponent's 10-yard line or 5-yard line.
